# Festival - Text to Speech, Speech Synthesis, and Voice Building

> **One calm speech workspace: read text aloud, shape voice, and build custom speakers.**

## Overview

Festival turns scattered speech tasks into a clear engine. With Festival text to speech and Festival speech synthesis workflows in mind, teams can read text, shape voice, and review output with less guesswork than juggling many tools.

## Why This Exists

Most speech tools need heavy installs and closed voices for simple reads. **Festival** solves this fragmentation by offering an open engine with buildable voices.

Festival solves **one specific problem**: open speech without closed voices. Just use Festival text to speech, run Festival speech synthesis, and build with Festival voice building without extra steps.

If you are tired of closed engines and rigid voices, **Festival** is for you.

Searches such as Festival text to speech, Festival speech synthesis, and Festival voice building usually point to the same goal: open speech anywhere. Festival keeps that process clear. Enter text, pick a voice, and confirm the result in the playback.
